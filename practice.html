Practice

Make each session answer one question.

A structured practice method for braking, cornering, throttle control, drifting and consistency.

Build the session in stages

Warm up

Drive a few ordinary runs to reconnect with the car. Do not judge the whole setup from the first attempt.

Choose one skill

Pick braking, entry, throttle control, drift initiation, angle control or transitions. A narrow target makes improvement easier to recognize.

Repeat a short section

Five to ten focused attempts can teach more than repeatedly driving a whole track without a target. Keep the setup stable during the first set.

Identify the repeated mistake

Look for the problem that appears most often. One unusual error may be an outlier; a repeated error gives you something to investigate.

Test one change

Change one driving input or one setup variable, then repeat the same section.

Finish with normal runs

Drive naturally afterward. This helps show whether the skill works when you are no longer thinking about every individual input.

Keep four notes: skill, section, repeated mistake and the next thing to test.
